**Movie Name:** Extraction

- Extraction (2020) follows a black-market mercenary hired to rescue the kidnapped son of an imprisoned Indian drug lord from the heavily guarded city of Dhaka, Bangladesh. The mission goes wrong when a double-cross forces the mercenary to fight through corrupt police and ruthless gangsters alone to keep the boy alive.

## Plot Breakdown
- **The Kidnapping:** Teenager Ovi Mahajan Jr. is abducted in Mumbai by thugs working for Amir Asif, a rival drug lord in Dhaka. Ovi's imprisoned father orders his loyal former special forces bodyguard, Saju Rav, to retrieve the boy without paying a ransom.

- **The Rescue:** Lacking the funds to pay mercenaries, Saju hires Tyler Rake and his team under false pretenses. Tyler infiltrates Dhaka, eliminates Ovi's captors, and successfully extracts the boy.

- **The Double-Cross:** Before Tyler can reach the extraction point, Saju ambushes his team and kills them to avoid paying the fee, intending to take Ovi back himself. Meanwhile, Amir Asif uses his influence over the Bangladeshi police and military to lock down the entire city.

- **The Escape:** Tyler refuses to abandon Ovi after discovering he will not be paid, likely due to lingering grief over his own late son. Tyler and Ovi evade elite police units, corrupt officers, and gangsters while moving through the locked-down streets.

- **The Alliance:** After a brutal hand-to-hand fight between Tyler and Saju, both men realize they cannot escape Dhaka alone. They form a reluctant alliance to get Ovi across the heavily fortified Buriganga River bridge to Tyler's handler, Nik Khan.

- **The Climax:** Saju sacrifices himself to clear a path for Ovi during a massive firefight on the bridge. Tyler draws enemy fire so Ovi can reach safety, taking multiple gunshot wounds and a shot to the neck before falling into the river.
